Amendia — DevOps diagrams

The deployment view: what runs, on which ports, talking to what, with which data persisted and which boundaries trusted.

Read-verified against the working tree on 2026-08-19. Companion to backend/docs/devops/Amendia_Component_Inventory_DevOps.xlsx. Each diagram is also a standalone .svg in this folder and renders in light and dark themes.

1 · Local deployment topology (docker compose)

Every container in the local stack, which compose file owns it, its host→container port mapping, and whether its data persists. Start here: it is the map the rest of the diagrams assume.

2 · Event & data flow

One durable topic exchange carries all asynchronous traffic. This shows who publishes, who consumes, the full routing-key vocabulary, the queue names — and, deliberately, what does not ride the bus (HTTP, MCP tool calls, third-party inference).

3 · Production topology (Helm chart 0.2.0)

What deploy/helm/amendia actually renders after the 2026-08-19 catch-up: services, PVC-backed datastores, Vault over Kubernetes auth, default-deny egress, and the conditional inference workload. Open items are listed on the diagram.

4 · Trust & exposure boundaries

Where authentication is enforced and where it is not. This is the diagram that makes CB-9 visible: glea-service is the only production service sitting in the unauthenticated zone, exposing the entire audit trail.
